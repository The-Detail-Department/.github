# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **leon@detail-department.com** with `SECURITY` in the subject line. Do not open a public issue.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think it reaches.

**What happens next**

| | |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | within one working day |
| Assessment and a plan | within five working days |
| Credit in the fix notes | if you want it |

## Scope

Every repository in this organisation.

## What our systems touch

We work inside client advertising accounts and delivery data. Integrations are read-only by default. Nothing changes inside a live account unless an approved receipt says so. Treat anything you reach as client data and stop.

## Please do not

- Access, change or delete data that is not yours
- Run scans that degrade a live service
- Social-engineer our people, our clients, or our suppliers
