# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be direct. Be accurate. Assume the other person is trying to get it right.

Criticise the work, never the person. "This breaks when the currency is not CHF" is useful. "This is careless" is not.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or publishing anyone's private information.

Sustained disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every repository, issue, pull request and discussion in this organisation, and to anyone representing the organisation in public.

## Enforcement

Report to **leon@detail-department.com**. Reports are read by a named person and answered within one working day.

Consequences run from a private word, to a temporary ban, to a permanent one. The response fits the behaviour.
